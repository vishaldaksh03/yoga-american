How it began

From one small room to a thriving community

Surya Yoga Shala began in 2012 as a single rented room with twelve mats and one big dream.

Our founder, Ananya Sharma, returned from years of study in Rishikesh and Mysore with a wish to make authentic yoga feel accessible — not intimidating. She named the shala after Surya, the sun: a daily reminder that, no matter what yesterday held, we can always rise again.

Today we are a family of eighteen teachers and thousands of students, but the heart of the shala hasn't changed. Every class still begins with a breath, a smile and the belief that yoga belongs to everyone.

Our philosophy

What we believe in

Four simple values guide every class, every conversation and every decision we make.

Ahimsa · Kindness

We practise non-harm towards ourselves and others. No forcing, no judgement — only compassion.

Authenticity

Our teaching honours the roots of yoga — asana, pranayama, meditation and philosophy.

Inclusivity

Every age, body, background and ability is welcome. Yoga meets you exactly where you are.

Growth

We keep learning too — our teachers complete ongoing training every single year.
