How it began

From one small room to a thriving community

Cedar & Sun Yoga began in 2014 as a single rented room off South Lamar with twelve mats and one big dream.

Our founder, Lauren Whitaker, came home to Texas after years of teaching in New York and studying in Mysore, India, with a wish to make yoga feel accessible — not intimidating. She named the studio for the cedar-covered hills west of town and the sun that rises over them: a daily reminder that, no matter what yesterday held, we can always begin again.

Today we are a team of eighteen teachers and thousands of students, but the heart of the studio hasn't changed. Every class still begins with a breath, a smile and the belief that yoga belongs to everyone.

Our philosophy

What we believe in

Four simple values guide every class, every conversation and every decision we make.

Kindness First

We practice ahimsa — non-harm — toward ourselves and others. No forcing, no judgment, only compassion.

Authenticity

We honor where yoga comes from — movement, breath, meditation and philosophy — and teach it with respect.

Inclusivity

Every age, body, background and ability is welcome. Yoga meets you exactly where you are.

Growth

We keep learning too — our teachers complete ongoing training every single year.

Our teachers

The hearts behind every class

Certified, experienced and endlessly passionate — our teachers are here to guide and support you.

Lauren Whitaker

Founder · Slow Flow & Meditation · E-RYT 500

20 years of practice, with training in New York and Mysore, India. Lauren's classes blend precise alignment with deep stillness.

Jess Calloway

Vinyasa Flow · RYT 500

A former dancer, Jess builds creative, musical flows set to great playlists. Loved for her Friday candlelight classes.

Marcus Bennett

Hot Power Flow · RYT 500

Hannah Brooks
