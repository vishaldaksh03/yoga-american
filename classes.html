Not sure where to start? Beginners Foundations or Hatha Flow are perfect first steps.

01

Hatha Flow

The classical foundation of yoga — steady postures, conscious breathing and deep relaxation.

Each pose is held for several breaths, giving you time to explore alignment and feel its effect. Ideal for building strength, flexibility and body awareness at a mindful pace.

⏱ 60 min🌿 All levels🔥 Gentle–moderate👤 Ananya Sharma
Book Hatha Flow
02

Vinyasa Flow

Breath-linked movement that flows like a dance from one posture to the next.

Creative sequences build internal heat, cardiovascular fitness and graceful strength. Expect sun salutations, standing sequences and a long, delicious Savasana.

⏱ 60–90 min🌿 Intermediate🔥 Dynamic👤 Meera Iyer
Book Vinyasa Flow
03

Yin & Restorative

Slow down, soften and let go. A deeply healing practice for stressful times.

Passive, floor-based postures are held for three to five minutes with bolsters and blankets, releasing deep connective tissue and calming the nervous system. Sunday's session ends with a sound bath.

⏱ 75–90 min🌿 All levels🔥 Soothing👤 Kavya Nair
Book Yin & Restorative
04

Meditation & Pranayama

Learn to master your breath — and your mind will follow.

Explore classical breathing techniques like Nadi Shodhana, Kapalbhati and Bhramari, followed by guided meditation. A powerful antidote to anxiety, poor sleep and a busy mind.

⏱ 45 min🌿 All levels🔥 Calm👤 Ananya Sharma
Book Meditation
05

Power Yoga

Strong, sweaty and empowering — yoga for athletes and adventurers.

Inspired by Ashtanga, this vigorous class builds core strength, endurance and focus with arm balances, inversions and challenging flows. Some yoga experience recommended.

⏱ 60–75 min🌿 Intermediate–Advanced🔥 Intense👤 Arjun Rao
Book Power Yoga
06

Beginners Foundations

Brand new to yoga? This is your safe, friendly starting point.

We break down the essential postures step by step, teach you how to breathe and use props, and build your confidence to join any class on the timetable. No flexibility required!

⏱ 45–60 min🌿 Beginner🔥 Easy👤 Kavya Nair

Before you come

Good to know

Arrive 10 minutes early

Give yourself time to check in, change and settle onto your mat. Doors close once class begins.

Practise on a light stomach

Avoid heavy meals for two hours before class. Bring a water bottle — we have a refill station.

Tell us about injuries

Let your teacher know about any injuries, pregnancy or health conditions so they can offer modifications.
