Questions

Frequently asked

Is my first class really free?

Yes! Every new student can try any regular class free of charge. Just book online or call us, and arrive 10 minutes early to fill in a short health form.

I'm not flexible. Can I still do yoga?

Absolutely. Flexibility is a result of yoga, not a requirement. Our Beginners Foundations and Hatha classes are designed for stiff bodies and complete newcomers.

What should I bring and wear?

Wear comfortable, stretchy clothes. We provide mats and all props free of charge. Bring a water bottle and, if you like, a small towel.

Can I pause or cancel my membership?

Yes. You can pause your membership for up to 2 months per year (for travel or illness), or cancel with 30 days' notice. Yearly plans can be transferred to a friend.

Do you offer student or senior discounts?

We offer 15% off all memberships for full-time students, seniors aged 60+ and members of the armed forces. Just bring a valid ID.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, all major debit and credit cards, net banking and cash at the front desk.
