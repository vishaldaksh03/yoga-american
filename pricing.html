Questions

Frequently asked

Is my first class really free?

Yes! Every new student can try any regular class free of charge. Just book online or call us, and arrive 10 minutes early to sign a quick liability waiver.

I'm not flexible. Can I still do yoga?

Absolutely. Flexibility is a result of yoga, not a requirement. Our Yoga Basics and Slow Flow classes are designed for stiff bodies and complete newcomers.

What should I bring and wear?

Wear comfortable, stretchy clothes. We provide mats and all props free of charge. Bring a water bottle and, for Hot Power Flow, a towel — or rent one at the front desk for $2.

Can I pause or cancel my membership?

Yes. You can pause your membership for up to 2 months per year (for travel, injury or illness), or cancel with 30 days' notice. Yearly plans can be transferred to a friend.

Do you offer student, senior or military discounts?

We offer 15% off all memberships for full-time students, teachers, seniors 65+, active military, veterans and first responders. Just bring a valid ID.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Memberships are billed automatically each month to the card on file.

What is your cancellation policy for classes?

You can cancel a booking free of charge up to 12 hours before class. Late cancellations and no-shows use the class credit; Unlimited and Premium members are charged a $10 fee so we can offer the spot to someone on the waitlist.
