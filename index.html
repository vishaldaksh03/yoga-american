About our shala

A sanctuary for body, mind & soul

Founded in 2012 by yoga teacher Ananya Sharma, Surya Yoga Shala was born from a simple wish: to share authentic, heart-centred yoga with everyone — whatever their age, shape or experience.

  • Certified teachers trained in Rishikesh & Mysore
  • Small classes with personal attention and hands-on guidance
  • All props provided — mats, blocks, bolsters and straps
  • Sun-filled studio, herbal tea lounge and changing rooms
