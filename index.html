About our studio

Your neighborhood studio for body, mind & soul

Founded in 2014 by Austin yoga teacher Lauren Whitaker, Cedar & Sun grew from a simple idea: great yoga should feel welcoming, not intimidating — whatever your age, shape or experience.

  • Yoga Alliance–registered teachers (RYT 200 & 500)
  • Small classes, capped at 24 mats, with real personal attention
  • Mats, blocks, bolsters and straps are always free to borrow
  • Sun-filled studio, tea lounge, showers and free parking
